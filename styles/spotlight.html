About

I'm a final-year Computer Science student on the Robotics & AI track at Helwan National University, and an IoT & AI developer at VoltX, where I build embedded ML pipelines on ESP32 and FreeRTOS.

Most of what I build runs live: hand tracking that switches video filters, a YOLOv11 safety detector, an ESP32 parking system with eight FreeRTOS tasks. I also lead a university team on a story-captioning system that checks its own captions for hallucinated objects.

Lately I'm deep in retrieval-augmented generation: a production-style telecom RAG API, and an Arabic legal assistant for Egyptian personal-status law. I care about the last mile, the part that turns a demo into a tool people use.
